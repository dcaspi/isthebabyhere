## Footnotes

1. **Arm** never had a traditional VC round — SoftBank took it private for $32B in 2016. The ~$64B mark comes from SoftBank buying back the Vision Fund's ~25% stake in Aug 2023, a month before the IPO.
2. **Instacart** marked itself down internally several times after the $39B round — to ~$24B, then ~$13B, then ~$10B by late 2022. The IPO priced near those markdowns, at ~25% of the peak round.
3. **Instacart's** share price ($42.20) is well above its $30 IPO price, but heavy buybacks shrank the share count to ~235M, so the market cap is roughly the same ~$10B as at IPO.
4. **Circle's** widely cited $9B figure was the revised (and terminated) 2022 SPAC deal; its last actual primary raise was the $7.7B Series F.
5. **Circle** peaked at a $263.45 close ($298.99 intraday) on June 23, 2025 — roughly $50B+ — before pulling back. The one-month figure is approximate.
6. **Figma** also had an agreed $20B acquisition by Adobe (Sep 2022), terminated on regulatory grounds in Dec 2023.

**Cross-reading with the valuation table:** profitability at IPO had almost no relationship to post-IPO performance. The two biggest money-losers at IPO (CoreWeave −$863M, Rubrik −$354M) are among the best performers since, while profitable Instacart and breakeven Chime/Klaviyo stagnated or fell. The market paid for growth and AI exposure, not earnings.

## Takeaways

- **Pop-and-fade is the dominant pattern.** Almost every name closed IPO day well above pricing, then gave much of it back within a month (Arm, Instacart, Klaviyo, Reddit, Figma, Chime, Circle all faded; CoreWeave was the exception — flat debut, then up).
- **First-day close is a terrible predictor of where the stock is a year+ later — in both directions.** Figma closed day one at ~$68B and now sits at ~$14.3B (below even its IPO pricing, near its 2024 private tender). Arm closed at ~$65B and is now ~$437B.
- **Vintage matters.** The 2021 mega-round class (Chime $25B, Klaviyo $9.5B, Instacart $39B) all still trade well below their peak private marks 5 years later. Companies that priced realistically off fresh marks (CoreWeave, Rubrik, Reddit relative to 2021) have been the strong performers.
- **AI exposure is the dividing line.** The big winners since IPO — Arm (~8x from pricing), CoreWeave (~3x), Reddit (~5x), Rubrik (~3x) — all ride AI demand (chips, GPU cloud, data licensing, data security). The losers — Figma, Chime, Klaviyo, Instacart — are app-layer software/fintech/consumer names facing AI disruption narratives or growth compression.
